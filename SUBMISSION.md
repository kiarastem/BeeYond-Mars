# BeeYond Mars — submission text (draft)

**High-level summary**
BeeYond Mars is a bilingual 8-bit exploration game where 1–4 players walk through a small town, gather a team and design a spacecraft to carry bumblebee queens in diapause to a Mars greenhouse. Players trade mass, power, delta-v and budget across real NASA Trajectory Browser routes, pass JPL-style design reviews (CML 1–4), react to timed in-flight events under signal delay, and receive a mission paper whose conclusions and peer review change with their decisions. Failing is part of the loop: the paper says what to fix, and the team redesigns.

**How it addresses the challenge**
It turns the mission-design cycle into play: define a payload, pick a launcher and trajectory, check constraints, fly, review, iterate. Each role (engineering, biology, communications, mission lead) owns part of the catalogue, so a group has to negotiate one shared budget.

**NASA and partner data**
NASA Ames Trajectory Browser (routes, dates, delta-v), a GMAT propagation of both routes made by the team (daily Sun and Earth distance, used for solar power and signal delay), JPL concept maturity levels, NASA Deep Space Network, NASA Mars facts (sunlight), STS-41-C honeybee experiment, NASA planetary protection policy, ESA Mars Express signal delay. Bumblebee diapause values come from peer-reviewed studies. All sources are listed inside the game.

**Use of AI**
Pixel art is AI-assisted and marked "AI" on every image. Code and text were developed with AI assistance (Claude) and reviewed by the team. No NASA branding is used; Aurelia is a fictional agency.

**Limits**
The model is simplified and hypothetical. Whether bumblebee queens can survive a trip to Mars is an open question; the game says so on the letter, in the report and in the sources panel.
